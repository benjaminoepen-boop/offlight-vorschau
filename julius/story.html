Our Story

OFFLINE + LIGHT ON = OFFLIGHT

We started with a small, annoying observation.

Nobody in our studio could sit through one film, one dinner, one chapter without reaching for a phone. Not because we wanted to. Because it was there.

So we built the object we wanted on our own desks: a beautiful box with somewhere for the phone to go, and a candle that says the moment has started. Lacquered ash, felt lining, coconut-soy wax. Designed in Switzerland, made in small runs.

We're not anti-technology. We just think it should wait its turn.

FAQ

  • Does it fit my phone?

    Every phone up to 6.9 inches, case on.

  • Is it safe to burn on the lid?

    Yes. The lid is a heat-safe pedestal, designed for it.

  • How long does the candle last?

    Around 45 hours. Refills are CHF 22.

  • Does it lock?

    No. It's a ritual, not a safe.
